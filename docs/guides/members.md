# Member guide

This guide is for SHPE @ CMU members and supporters who use [shpecmu.com](https://shpecmu.com).
A free account lets you choose which chapter newsletters you get and RSVP to events. Anyone 13 or
older can create one.

## Creating an account

### How to join

1. Go to [shpecmu.com/join](/join), or choose **Join** at the bottom of any page.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email** and a **Password**. The password needs at least 12 characters.
   Type it again in **Confirm password**.
3. Tick **I am 13 or older**.
4. Read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (linked under the box), then tick **I agree to the
   Terms of Use and Privacy Policy**.
5. Choose **Create account**.

You see "Check your inbox". Your account isn't ready until you confirm your email.

![The Create an account form with the two checkboxes and the Create account button](/help/img/members/join.png)

![The Check your inbox page](/help/img/members/check-email.png)

### How to confirm your email

1. Open the email from SHPE @ CMU and choose the confirmation link. The link works for 24 hours.
2. On the **Confirm your email** page, enter the **Password** you chose when you signed up.
3. Choose **Confirm my email**.

You are taken to the sign-in page with the message "Your email is confirmed — sign in to continue."

![The Confirm your email page with the Password field and the Confirm my email button](/help/img/members/confirm-email.png)

### If the email doesn't arrive

1. Wait a few minutes and check your spam or junk folder.
2. If it still isn't there, go to [shpecmu.com/join/resend](/join/resend). The sign-in page also
   links to it as **Didn't get the confirmation email?**
3. Enter your **Email** and choose **Send a new link**. Older links stop working.

If you see "This link doesn't work", the link has expired, was already used, or was cut off when
you copied it. Choose **Send me a new confirmation link**.

Accounts that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. You can simply join again.

## Signing in and out

### How to sign in

1. Choose **Sign in** at the bottom of any page.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.

You land on **Your account**. While you are signed in, the top bar shows your name, an
**Account** link and **Sign out**. On a phone, open the menu to see them.

If you see "Invalid email or password.", check both and try again. After several wrong tries
you have to wait 15 minutes.

### How to sign out

Choose **Sign out** in the top bar.

## Passwords

### If you forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email** and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and choose the link. It works for 1 hour.
4. Enter a **New password**, type it again in **Confirm new password**, and choose **Save new
   password**.

Choosing a new password signs you out on every other device.

![The Forgot your password page with the Send reset link button](/help/img/members/forgot-password.png)

### How to change your password

1. Sign in and go to **Your account** (choose **Account** in the top bar).
2. Under **Change password**, enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** and
   **Confirm new password**.
3. Choose **Change password**.

You stay signed in on this device and are signed out everywhere else.

## Your account page

Choose **Account** in the top bar to open **Your account**. From there you can:

- change your name under **Your name**, then choose **Save name**;
- choose your newsletters (see below);
- see and cancel your RSVPs under **My RSVPs**;
- change your password;
- delete your account.

![The Your account page with the Newsletters section](/help/img/members/account.png)

### Changing your email address

You can't change the email address of an account. To use a different address:

1. Create a new account with the new address and confirm it.
2. Choose your newsletters again in the new account.
3. Sign in to the old account and delete it (see [Deleting your account](#deleting-your-account)).

If you are a sponsor contact, ask a chapter officer to link your new account to your company's
listing.

## Newsletters

### How to choose your newsletters

1. Go to **Your account**.
2. Under **Newsletters**, tick each topic you want to hear about in **Email me about**, and untick
   the ones you don't.
3. Choose **Save newsletter choices**.

You see "Your newsletter choices were saved." You get only one copy of a newsletter, however many
of its topics you chose.

### How to unsubscribe

Every newsletter has an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom.

1. Choose **Unsubscribe** in the email. You don't need to sign in.
2. On the page that opens, choose **Unsubscribe from** followed by the topic names to stop the
   topics this email was sent for. Or choose **Unsubscribe from all newsletters** to stop all of
   them.

Nothing changes until you press one of the buttons, and it takes effect right away. Your email
app's own unsubscribe button works too: it stops the topics that newsletter was sent for.

You can also untick topics at any time under **Newsletters** on **Your account**, and tick them
again to resubscribe.

![The unsubscribe page with the two unsubscribe buttons](/help/img/members/unsubscribe.png)

## Events and RSVPs

### How to RSVP to an event

1. Sign in, then open the **Events** page.
2. Find the event under upcoming events and choose **RSVP**.

You see "You’re going. See you there!" If the event has a limit, it shows how many spots are left.
A full event shows **Full**, and you can no longer RSVP to it. If you aren't signed in, the event
shows **Sign in to RSVP** instead.

![An upcoming event with the spots left and the RSVP button](/help/img/members/events-rsvp.png)

### How to cancel an RSVP

Cancel before the event so someone else can take your place. Either:

- open the **Events** page and choose **Cancel RSVP** on the event; or
- go to **Your account** and choose **Cancel RSVP** next to the event under **My RSVPs**.

**My RSVPs** lists the upcoming events you are going to. You can't cancel once the event has
happened.

![The My RSVPs section with the Cancel RSVP button](/help/img/members/my-rsvps.png)

## Deleting your account

1. Go to **Your account** and scroll to **Delete my account**.
2. Enter **Your password**.
3. Type DELETE in capital letters in **Type DELETE to confirm**.
4. Choose **Delete my account**.

Your account, newsletter choices and RSVPs are deleted right away and you are signed out. This
can't be undone. You can join again later with the same email.

![The Delete my account section](/help/img/members/delete-account.png)

## Privacy and terms

The **Privacy** and **Terms** links at the bottom of every page open the chapter's Privacy
Policy ([shpecmu.com/privacy](/privacy)) and Terms of Use ([shpecmu.com/terms](/terms)). They
explain what we collect, why, how long we keep it and your choices.

## Getting help

For anything else, use the **Contact** page or email the address shown at the bottom of every
page.
