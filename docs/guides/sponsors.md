# Sponsor guide

Thank you for supporting SHPE at Colorado Mesa University. This guide is for company contacts who
use the sponsor portal on [shpecmu.com](https://shpecmu.com). In the portal you can keep your
company's listing up to date, RSVP to chapter events and download your receipts.

## Getting access

Sponsor access is given by a chapter officer. It is linked to your own account, not shared by your
company.

1. Create a free account at [shpecmu.com/join](/join) with your work email. The
   [Member guide](/help/members#creating-an-account) explains each step.
2. Confirm your email: open the email from SHPE @ CMU, choose the link, enter your password and
   choose **Confirm my email**. Officers can't give access to an account that isn't confirmed.
3. Tell your chapter contact the email address you signed up with.
4. An officer links your account to your company's listing.

A listing can have several sponsor accounts, so colleagues can each have their own. Each account
can be linked to only one listing.

## Signing in

1. Choose **Sign in** at the bottom of any page on [shpecmu.com](https://shpecmu.com).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.

Once your access is set up, you land on the **Sponsor portal**. It shows "Signed in as" followed by
your email. You can come back to it any time from **Account** in the top bar, then **Sponsor
portal**.

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page; see the
[Member guide](/help/members#if-you-forgot-your-password).

![The top of the Sponsor portal with the Signed in as line](/help/img/sponsors/portal.png)

## Editing your listing

Your listing is how your company appears on the public Sponsors page. Under **Your listing** you
can change:

- **Name**
- **Website (optional)**: a full link starting with https://
- **Description (optional)**: up to 1000 characters
- the logo, with **Upload logo (optional)** or **Replace logo**
- **Logo background color**: the colour of the tile behind your logo

### How to update your listing

1. Change the fields you want.
2. To change the logo, choose **Upload logo (optional)** or **Replace logo** and pick an image
   file under 8 MB. In the **Frame this logo** window, pick a shape, then drag and use the **Zoom** and
   rotate controls until it looks right, and choose **Use this framing**.
3. Choose **Save listing**.

You see "Your listing was saved." Changes appear on the public Sponsors page straight away.

Your tier and your position on the page are set by the chapter. Ask your chapter contact if
either needs to change.

Every saved change is recorded with your name, the time and which fields changed. Chapter officers
can see this history.

![The Your listing form with the Save listing button](/help/img/sponsors/edit-listing.png)

## Events and RSVPs

**Upcoming events** in the portal lists the chapter's upcoming events with their date, time and
location.

- Choose **RSVP** to say you're coming. You see "You’re going. See you there!"
- If an event has a limit, it shows how many spots are left. A full event shows **Full**.
- To cancel, choose **Cancel RSVP** on the event, or next to it under **My RSVPs** further down.

Please cancel if your plans change, so someone else can take your place.

![The Upcoming events list in the Sponsor portal with an RSVP button](/help/img/sponsors/events.png)

## Downloading receipts

When the chapter shares a receipt with your company, it appears under **Receipts**, with its
title and date.

1. Choose **Download** next to the receipt. The link also shows the file size.
2. The PDF is saved to your device.

Receipts are private. Only chapter officers and the accounts linked to your listing can download
them.

![The Receipts section with a Download link](/help/img/sponsors/receipts.png)

## If you don't have access

If signing in takes you to **Your account** instead of the Sponsor portal, and there is no
**Sponsor portal** button there, your account isn't linked to a listing. This can happen when:

- your account was just created and an officer hasn't linked it yet;
- you signed up with a different email address from the one you gave the chapter;
- the chapter changed or removed your company's listing.

Contact the chapter through the **Contact** page, or at the email address shown at the bottom of
every page. Give the email address you signed in with and your company's name.

If you see "Your sponsor access changed while you were editing, so nothing was saved.", your access
changed while you were editing. Reload the page and check with the chapter.

## Privacy and terms

Your account follows the same Privacy Policy ([shpecmu.com/privacy](/privacy)) and Terms of Use
([shpecmu.com/terms](/terms)) as every member account. The **Privacy** and **Terms** links are at the
bottom of every page.
